WHY DATA ENGINEERING

A role top product companies pay ₹30+ LPA for.

Every product company with real scale — fintech, e-commerce, SaaS — runs on data pipelines someone has to design, build and keep alive. That someone is a data engineer, and demand for the role has outpaced supply for years.
